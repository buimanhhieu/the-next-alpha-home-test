Keep your billing details up to date from **Payment Settings**: your billing email, payment method, billing address and tax ID. Your past invoices and receipts are under **Billing History**.

---

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account with access to billing (the account Owner or an Admin)

---

Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of the portal and select **Payment Settings**. It sits under **Billing & Orders** in the settings menu on the left. You can also go straight to [app.optisigns.com/app/s/payment](https://app.optisigns.com/app/s/payment).

The page has a collapsible section for each part of your billing details:

 
-  **Billing Email** - Where billing emails are sent. 
-  **Billing Information** - Your payment method. 
-  **Billing Address** - The address on your invoices. 
-  **Tax ID** - Your VAT or other tax ID. Shown only when your billing address is outside the US. 
-  **Discount** - A coupon code for your subscription. 
Click a section's title to expand or collapse it.

---

## Change Your Billing Email
In **Billing Email**, click **Update Email**. Type the new address and click **Save**.

---

## Add or Change Your Payment Method
In **Billing Information**, you'll see the card on file, or "No payment method on file". Click **Update Payment Method**.

Choose **Card** or **US bank account**. For a card, fill in **Card number**, **Expiration date**, **Security code**, **Country** and **ZIP code**, then click **Save**.

---

## Add or Change Your Billing Address
Expand **Billing Address** and click **Update Billing Address**. Fill in **Address**, **City**, **Country**, **State** and **Zip / Postal Code**. **Address Line 2** and **Phone** are optional. Click **Save**.

---

## Add or Change Your Tax ID
If you're outside the US and need a VAT invoice, add your VAT ID here.

Expand **Tax ID** and click **Update Tax**. Click **+ Add another ID**, choose the **ID type**, type your tax ID, and click the green tick to save it. To remove an ID, click the red bin next to it. You can add up to 5 tax IDs.

---

## Apply a Coupon Code
Expand **Discount**, enter your coupon code and click **Apply**.

---

## View Billing History and Receipts
Click your profile icon in the top-right corner and select **Billing History** (under **Billing & Orders** in the settings menu), or go to [app.optisigns.com/app/s/payment-history](https://app.optisigns.com/app/s/payment-history).

**Invoice History** lists your upcoming charge and your past invoices, with the **Date**, **Invoice ID**, **Amount** and **Status** of each.

 
- To see what your next invoice will charge, click the view button on the upcoming charge. 
- To get a receipt, click **View receipt** on a paid invoice. The receipt opens in a new tab, where you can download it. 

---

## Troubleshooting
Here are some of the more common questions and issues you might face when updating your billing information, and their solutions.

#### I don't see the Tax ID section
The section appears only when your billing address is outside the US. See [Add or Change Your Billing Address](#AddOrChangeYourBillingAddress).

#### "You do not have access to this page"
Billing pages are only available to users who manage billing for the account. Ask your account Owner or an Admin.

#### "Invalid coupon code"
Check the code for typos and that it hasn't expired. If it still doesn't work, contact [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com).
