# How to Use the Follow Us App
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049875794-How-to-Use-the-Follow-Us-App

### In this article, we'll show how to invite viewers to follow your social media accounts from your digital signs with OptiSigns.
 
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed) 
- [Creating a Follow Us App](#CreatingAFollowUsApp) 
- [Deploying a Follow Us App](#DeployingAFollowUsApp) 
With the Follow Us app, it's possible to invite viewers to follow you on social media, right from your screens. Show your Facebook, Instagram or X profile with a scannable QR code, plus your website, email and phone number, and grow your follower base.

Let's get started.

---

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account - ****[Standard Plan or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)  
- The username of each social media profile you want to show 
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)  
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891)) 

---

## Creating a Follow Us App
From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

Search for **Follow Us** and select the **Follow Us** app.

The Follow Us app settings will open. Under **Social Profile**, choose **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **X**, then type the profile's username in the box next to it. For example, for https://www.instagram.com/optisigns, enter **optisigns**. The link under the box shows the full profile address, so you can check for typos.

To show more than one profile, click **Add more**. To remove one, click the **X (cancel button)** next to it.

Then fill in the rest of the settings:

 
-  **Name** - The name of your Follow Us asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens. 
-  **Title** - The text shown on your screen above the profile, such as "Follow us for the latest news." 
-  **Social Profile** - The Facebook, Instagram or X profiles to show. Add one, or add several. 
-  **Theme** - Choose **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**. 
-  **Speed** - If you've added more than one profile, this sets how long each profile shows before moving to the next. Choose **Slow**, **Medium** or **Fast**, or **Custom** to set your own speed. 
-  **Website** (optional) - A website address to show on the screen. 
-  **Email** (optional) - An email address to show on the screen. 
-  **Telephone** (optional) - A phone number to show on the screen. 
When **Speed** is set to **Custom**, drag the slider to choose a speed from 1 to 20.

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Follow Us app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

---

## Deploying a Follow Us App
To push your Follow Us app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Follow Us app, click it, then hit **Select**.

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Follow Us app will now display on your screen.

Your Follow Us app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
