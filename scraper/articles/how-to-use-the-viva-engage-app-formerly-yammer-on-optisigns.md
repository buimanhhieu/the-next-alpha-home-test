# How to Use the Viva Engage app (formerly Yammer) on OptiSigns
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/19171222981523-How-to-Use-the-Viva-Engage-app-formerly-Yammer-on-OptiSigns

You can display your Yammer Posts on your digital signs screen with OptiSigns.

 

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: [http://app.optisigns.com/](http://app.optisigns.com/)
