# LG webOS Smart TVs (Consumer Grade)
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/56946344474003-LG-webOS-Smart-TVs-Consumer-Grade

### In this article, we'll install the OptiSigns app on an LG webOS Smart TV and pair it with your OptiSigns account.

You can run OptiSigns on an LG webOS Smart TV, so your signage plays straight from the TV without any extra hardware.

This article is for consumer LG Smart TVs. If you have a commercial LG display that runs webOS Signage, see our [LG webOS Signage article](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4414496579475) instead.

---

## Before You Start
 
- Connect the TV to the internet. A wired connection is best, because OptiSigns streams your content. 
- Update the TV's software from its **Settings** menu. 

---

## Install the OptiSigns App
Press the **Home** button on the remote and open the **LG Content Store**. Search for **OptiSigns**.

Select the OptiSigns app, then select **Install**.

When it finishes, open OptiSigns from the home screen.

The app shows a pairing code. Keep it on screen; you'll need it to pair.

---

## Pair the TV with Your Account

1. On a computer or phone, go to [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/) and log in. If you don't have an account yet, select **Create Account**. You can also log in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook.

1. On the **Screens** page, select **Add Screen**.

1. Enter the pairing code shown on your TV in **Pair code**, then select **Pair**.

**Note:** Codes are case-sensitive, and the portal enters them in capitals. If the code on your TV has lowercase letters, select **Use lowercase pairing code** and type it exactly as shown.

---

## Open OptiSigns on Startup
Since LG webOS TVs don't start apps automatically, open OptiSigns from the home screen each time the TV turns on. Once it's open, it plays whatever you've assigned to the screen in the portal.
