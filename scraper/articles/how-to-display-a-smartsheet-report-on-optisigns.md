# How to Display a Smartsheet Report on OptiSigns
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/8399990754195-How-to-Display-a-Smartsheet-Report-on-OptiSigns

Smartsheet is a popular SaaS platform for project & work management.

With OptiSigns, you can easily display your Smartsheet reports and dashboards on your screens.

 

 

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891-OptiSigns-Getting-Started-Guide).
