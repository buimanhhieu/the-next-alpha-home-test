# How to Use the Taggbox Display App
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009343862-How-to-Use-the-Taggbox-Display-App

### In this article, we'll show how to display your Taggbox social wall on your digital signs with OptiSigns.
 
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed) 
- [Getting Your Taggbox Display Link](#GettingYourTaggboxDisplayLink) 
- [Creating a Taggbox Display App](#CreatingATaggboxDisplayApp) 
- [Deploying a Taggbox Display App](#DeployingATaggboxDisplayApp) 
With the Taggbox Display app, it's possible to show a [Taggbox](https://taggbox.com/) social wall on your screens. Taggbox collects content from 15+ social platforms, such as Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, and lets you customize and moderate your wall. It also supports video, which some of our built-in social apps don't.

Let's get started.

---

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account - ****[Standard Plan or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)  
- A [Taggbox](https://taggbox.com/) account with a social wall set up. Taggbox has a [free plan](https://taggbox.com/price/widget-price/) for a single feed 
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)  
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891)) 

---

## Getting Your Taggbox Display Link
Sign in to [Taggbox](https://taggbox.com/) and open your wall. Go to its display options and copy the wall's **Display Link**.

---

## Creating a Taggbox Display App
From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

Search for **Taggbox** and select the **Taggbox Display** app.

The Taggbox Display app settings will open. 

Fill them in:

 
-  **Name** - The name of your Taggbox Display asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens. 
-  **URL** - Paste your Taggbox Display Link here. The link should start with https://display.socialwalls.com/ or https://app.taggbox.com/. 
-  **Update Interval** - How often, in seconds, the app checks your wall for changes. The default is **600** (10 minutes). Enter **0** to check only when the app first loads. 
Now hit **Save**. 

You'll have created a Taggbox Display app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

---

## Deploying a Taggbox Display App
To push your Taggbox Display app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Taggbox Display app, click it, then hit **Select**.

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Taggbox wall will now display on your screen.

Your Taggbox Display app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
