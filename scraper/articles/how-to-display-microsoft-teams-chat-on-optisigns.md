# How to Display Microsoft Teams Chat on OptiSigns
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417940565139-How-to-Display-Microsoft-Teams-Chat-on-OptiSigns

This app is to display Microsoft Teams Chat. We have other apps to display [Microsoft Team Meetings](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404053129619) or [Team Live events](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411530012307).

You can display your Microsoft Teams Channels on your digital signs screen with OptiSigns.

---
First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891-OptiSigns-Getting-Started-Guide).

On our OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets > Apps** then find **Microsoft Teams**.
