# How to Use the Notion App
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/5119724468371-How-to-Use-the-Notion-App

### In this article, we'll show how to display your Notion pages on your digital signs with OptiSigns.
 
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed) 
- [Creating a Notion App](#CreatingANotionApp) 
- [Deploying a Notion App](#DeployingANotionApp) 
With the Notion app, it's possible to display any Notion page on your digital signs. Share team notes, announcements, menus or project updates straight from Notion, and let your screens refresh as the page changes.

Let's get started.

---

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account - ****[Standard Plan or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)  
- A Notion account, with permission to share the page you want to display to the web 
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)  
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891)) 

---

## Creating a Notion App
First, open the page you want to display in Notion. Click **Share**, turn on **Share to web**, then hit **Copy** next to the public link.

Next, go to the OptiSigns portal. Go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

Search for **Notion** and select the **Notion** app.

The Notion app settings will open. Fill in the following:

 
-  **Name** - The name of your Notion asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens. 
-  **URL** - The public Notion link you copied in the first step. 
-  **Update Interval** - How often the app checks Notion for changes, measured in seconds. The default is 600 (every 10 minutes). Set it to 0 to check only when the app first loads. 

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Notion app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

---

## Deploying a Notion App
To push your Notion app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Notion app, click it, then hit **Select**.

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Notion page will now display on your screen.

Your Notion app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
