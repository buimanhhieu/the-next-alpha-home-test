# How to Use the Twitch App
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046859534-How-to-Use-the-Twitch-App

### In this article, we'll show how to display live Twitch streams on your digital signs with OptiSigns.
 
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed) 
- [Creating a Twitch App](#CreatingATwitchApp) 
- [Deploying a Twitch App](#DeployingATwitchApp) 
Twitch is home to far more than game streaming. Channels cover music, talk shows, sports, creative work and live classes. With the Twitch app, it's possible to play any public Twitch channel on your digital signs, with or without the live chat beside it.

Let's get started.

---

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account - ****[Standard Plan or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)  
- The link to the Twitch channel you want to display 
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)  
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891)) 

---

## Creating a Twitch App
First, go to **twitch.tv** and open the channel you want to display. Copy the channel's address from your browser's address bar, for example **https://www.twitch.tv/twitch**.

Next, go to the OptiSigns portal. Go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

Search for **Twitch** and select the **Twitch** app.

The Twitch app settings will open. Fill in the following:

 
-  **Name** - The name of your Twitch asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens. 
-  **Twitch URL** - The channel address you copied in the first step. 
-  **Theme** - **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**. This mainly changes the look of the chat box. 
-  **Chat Box** - Shows the channel's live chat next to the stream. It's on by default; turn it off to play the stream full screen. 

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Twitch app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

---

## Deploying a Twitch App
To push your Twitch app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Twitch app, click it, then hit **Select**.

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Twitch stream will now play on your screen.

Your Twitch app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
