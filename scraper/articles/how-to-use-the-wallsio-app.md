# How to Use the Walls.io App
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049159814-How-to-Use-the-Walls-io-App

### In this article, we'll show how to display your Walls.io social media wall on your digital signs with OptiSigns.
 
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed) 
- [Creating a Walls.io App](#CreatingAWallsIoApp) 
- [Deploying a Walls.io App](#DeployingAWallsIoApp) 
With the Walls.io app, it's possible to show your Walls.io social media wall on your screens. Collect posts from your social channels and hashtags in Walls.io, then display them on your digital signs by pasting in your wall's URL.

Let's get started.

---

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account - ****[Standard Plan or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)  
- A [Walls.io](https://walls.io/) account with a social wall set up 
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)  
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891)) 

---

## Creating a Walls.io App
First, open your wall in Walls.io and copy its URL from your browser's address bar.

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

Search for **Walls.io** and select the **Walls.io** app.

The Walls.io app settings will open. Fill in the settings:

 
-  **Name** - The name of your Walls.io asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens. 
-  **URL** - Paste the URL of your Walls.io wall here, for example https://my.walls.io/your-wall-name. 
-  **Update Interval** - How often, in seconds, OptiSigns checks your wall for updates. Walls.io updates your wall automatically, so this doesn't need to be low. We recommend the default of 600 seconds. 

If you'd rather not show the Walls.io brand bar at the top of your wall, use your wall's embed code with our [Embed App](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035815114) instead.

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Walls.io app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**. It may take a few seconds for your wall to load.

---

## Deploying a Walls.io App
To push your Walls.io app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Walls.io app, click it, then hit **Select**.

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Walls.io app will now display on your screen.

Your Walls.io app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
