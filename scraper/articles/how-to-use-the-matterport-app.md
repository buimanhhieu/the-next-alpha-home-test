# How to Use the Matterport App
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/6091289267475-How-to-Use-the-Matterport-App

### In this article, we'll show how to display Matterport 3D tours on your digital signs with OptiSigns.
 
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed) 
- [Creating a Matterport App](#CreatingAMatterportApp) 
- [Deploying a Matterport App](#DeployingAMatterportApp) 
Matterport turns real spaces into interactive 3D tours. With the Matterport app, it's possible to show those tours on your digital signs, so you can showcase a property, venue, store or facility in your lobby, showroom or sales office.

Let's get started.

---

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account - ****[Standard Plan or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)  
- The link to the Matterport tour you want to display 
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)  
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891)) 

---

## Creating a Matterport App
First, open the Matterport tour you want to display and copy its link. It will look like: 

https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=tyEb2t1RWsc.

Next, go to the OptiSigns portal. Go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

Search for **Matterport** and select the **Matterport** app.

The Matterport app settings will open. Fill in the following:

 
-  **Name** - The name of your Matterport asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens. 
-  **URL** - The Matterport tour link you wish to display. 

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Matterport app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

---

## Deploying a Matterport App
To push your Matterport app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Matterport app, click it, then hit **Select**.

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Matterport tour will now display on your screen.

Your Matterport app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
