# How to Fix the Scaling Issue on OptiSigns Android Devices
**Source:** https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/28026588373139-How-to-Fix-the-Scaling-Issue-on-OptiSigns-Android-Devices

### In this article, we'll show how to change the display size on your OptiSigns Android Stick so text and graphics show at the right scale, on every device generation.

If text, charts or web pages look too large or too small on your OptiSigns Android Stick, the device's display size setting is the cause. You can change it from the device's own Settings menu in a minute.

---

## What Scaling Looks Like
The display size controls how many dots per inch (dpi) Android draws with. A lower setting fits more on the screen; a higher one makes everything bigger. Here is the same web page at two settings.

## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns Android Stick connected to your screen 
- The remote that came with it, or a USB mouse 
The steps depend on your device's generation. If the home screen shows an **OptiSigns** tile, a large **Settings** tile and **Mix Apps**, you have a Gen 3 device. If it shows an **Apps** button, a row of app icons and a settings gear in the top-right corner, follow the steps for earlier devices.

---

## Change the Display Size on the Android Stick Gen 3
On the home screen, select **Settings**.

Select **Device Preferences**.

Select **Display & Sound**.

Select **Display Size**.

On the **Display Size** page, press left or right on your remote to make everything on the screen smaller or larger.

---

## Change the Display Size on Earlier Android Sticks
On the home screen, select the settings gear in the top-right corner.

Select **Android settings**.

Select **Extra settings**.

Select **Display**.

Select **Display size**. It shows the current setting, for example **Default 320dpi**.

Press right on your remote until the **-** button is highlighted, then use it and the **+** button to change the dpi.

---

## After You Change the Setting
Open the OptiSigns app again. Your content now shows at the new scale. If it still isn't right, repeat the steps and try one step smaller or larger.
