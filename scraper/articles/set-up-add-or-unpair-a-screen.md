## What You'll Need
 
- An OptiSigns account. If you don't have one, you can create one for free at [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/). 
- A player running the OptiSigns app, such as an Android, Fire TV, Windows, Mac or Linux device, connected to the internet 
- A screen connected to the player 

---

Download the OptiSigns app for your player from [optisigns.com/download](https://www.optisigns.com/download).

Once the app is installed, open it. It asks whether to start automatically when the device starts up. Select **Yes**, unless you want to open the app by hand every time.

The app then shows a pairing code:

## Pair the Screen with Your Account
On your computer, go to [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/) and sign in. You can also sign in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook, or with your company's SSO.

On the **Screens** page, click **Add Screen**.

Type the pairing code shown on your screen into **Pair code**, then click **Pair**.

Your screen changes to show that it's paired:

## Put Content on Your Screen
Go to **Files/Assets** and click **Upload Files**.

Drag files from your computer into the window, click **browse files**, or import them from Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and more. Then click **Upload**.

Once your files are uploaded, open the menu on a file and select **Push To Screens**. Select your screen and click **Push**. The file now plays on your screen.

## Change the Screen's Orientation
To show content in portrait, or to fix a screen that's mounted upside down, rotate the screen. The options are:

 
-  **Landscape** - No rotation. 
-  **Rotate 90 degrees **- Best for "Portrait" style displays. 
- **Rotate 180 degrees** 
- **Rotate 270 degrees** 
For the best result, use content made for portrait when you rotate by 90 or 270 degrees. OptiSigns apps are designed to fit portrait mode too.

### Rotate from the Portal
On the **Screens** page, click **Edit** on the screen. Choose a rotation from **Orientation** and click **Save**.

### Rotate from the Device
Open the OptiSigns app's side menu on the device, select **Rotation**, and choose an option.

---

## Unpair a Screen
Unpair a screen when you no longer use it, or to move it to a different account. You can do this from the portal or from the device.

### Unpair from the Portal
On the **Screens** page, click the three-dot menu at the end of the screen's row and select **Remove**.

Type **DELETE** to confirm and click **Delete**.

### Unpair from the Device
Open the OptiSigns app's side menu: press the menu button (three bars) on a Fire TV remote, or press **Enter** on a keyboard for Windows, Linux or Mac. Then select **Unpair Device**.

## Troubleshooting
Here are a few common issues people may find when they are setting up and adding a screen, and how to solve them.

#### "Please verify the code and try again. Codes are case-sensitive."
Check the code on your screen and type it exactly, matching upper and lower case. If the player shows a lowercase code, select **Use lowercase pairing code**.

#### Add Screen is greyed out
Your plan's screen limit has been reached, or your role can't add screens. Hover over the button to see why. To add more screens, upgrade your plan or ask your account Owner or an Admin.

#### "Free Plan does not support" this device type
Some player types need a paid plan. Click **upgrade your plan** in the message, or use another device.

The Free Plan is **ONLY **supported on these devices:

 
- OptiSigns Android Player 
- OptiSigns Pro or ProMax Player 
- OptiKiosk (any version) 
- Windows Devices 
- Linux Devices 
- Raspberry Pi 
- MacOS 
The OptiSigns Free Plan **IS NOT SUPPORTED ON:**

 
- Amazon Devices (FireStick, Amazon Signage Stick, FireTV) 
- Android Devices (including Android Smart TVs, Google TV, Chromecast with Google TV, and other Android-based players) 
- Roku 
- Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, etc.) 
- ChromeOS 
- AppleTV / iOS / iPad 
- BrightSign players
